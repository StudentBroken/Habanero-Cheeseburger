# Headset Mic Upgrade

## The Problem
Gaming headsets usually have terrible microphones. I liked the comfort of my current headset but hated the audio quality during calls. Instead of buying a new one, I decided to swap the cheap internal mic for a high-quality component.

## The Mod
I opened up the headset and removed the original microphone element.
- **New Part**: I soldered in a high-sensitivity electret mic capsule with better clarity and less background noise.
- **Integration**: I wired it directly to the headset’s internal cable so it still uses the original 3.5mm plug, with no extra wires or external batteries needed.
- **Mounting**: I glued the new mic into the existing boom arm so it looks almost stock.

## Result
The audio quality improved significantly. My voice is clear, and friends on Discord immediately noticed the difference. It’s a simple $5 fix that saved me from spending $150 on a new headset.
