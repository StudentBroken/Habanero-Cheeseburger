# Acoustic Transducer Retrofit: Headset Microphone

## Technical Objective
The objective was the engineering of a high-fidelity acoustic transducer retrofit for a standard gaming headset. The project aimed to replace the low-quality OEM microphone with a high-sensitivity electret capsule while maintaining a single-bus connector architecture.

## Hardware Specification & Integration
The modification involved the decommissioning of the internal microphone elements and the integration of a standalone electret microphone unit.
- **Acoustic Node**: High-sensitivity electret capsule, selected for its superior signal-to-noise ratio and frequency response compared to the OEM baseline.
- **Signal Integrity**: The capsule was wired directly to the headset's primary input/output lead, ensuring full integration with the existing signal chain without requiring secondary external cables.
- **Mechanical Mounting**: Rigid attachment to the existing boom arm, optimized for axial voice capture.

## Operational Conclusion
The retrofit successfully validated the "capsule-replacement" methodology for audio peripheral optimization. Post-modification tests confirmed a significant increase in vocal clarity and background noise rejection. The project demonstrates the utility of targeted hardware modifications over full-system replacement for high-value peripheral components.
