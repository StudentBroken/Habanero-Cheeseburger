# Integrated Acoustic Transducer: Helmet Retrofit

## Technical Objective
The objective was the integration of high-fidelity acoustic drivers into a standard alpine helmet's ear-protection assemblies. The project prioritized "zero-visibility" structural integration and internal cable management to maintain the OEM aerodynamic and aesthetic profile.

## Hardware Specification & Integration
The system utilizes compact acoustic drivers selected for their volumetric compatibility with the helmet's internal foam cavities.
- **Acoustic Node**: Discrete driver units, wired for stereo output.
- **Structural Routing**: All conductors were routed through pre-existing internal channels and secured behind the liner to ensure zero-snag operation.
- **Physical Interface**: Low-profile connector integrated at the helmet's base for external audio input.

## Operational Assessment
The retrofit successfully achieved the design target of zero external visibility. Acoustic performance was validated as sufficient for high-ambient-noise alpine environments. The internal routing architecture proved mechanically robust over multiple deployment cycles, confirming the viability of "discrete-retrofit" methodology for wearable audio.
