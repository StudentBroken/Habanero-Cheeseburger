# Micro FPV Drone

## The Goal
I wanted to see how small I could build a functional FPV drone. The goal was to make a quadcopter about the size of a LEGO minifigure that could still fly around the house and transmit video.

## Custom Shrouds
Because the props are so tiny and fragile, any bump against a wall would break them. I 3D printed custom shrouds to protect the rotors.
- **Design**: Lightweight PLA guards that clip onto the frame.
- **Purpose**: These allowed me to crash into walls and keep flying, which is essential for indoor flying.

## Specs
- **Size**: About 4cm wide.
- **Camera**: All-in-one FPV camera and transmitter.
- **Flight**: Micro flight controller and brushed motors.

## Result
It flew well indoors and was stable enough to navigate through tight spaces. I eventually lost it when I tried flying outside—it was so small and light that a gust of wind carried it away into the grass. But it proved that micro-scale FPV is feasible.
