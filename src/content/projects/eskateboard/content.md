# Propeller-Driven Skateboard

## The Idea

I wanted to build an electric skateboard on a minimal budget. Instead of belts or hub motors, I explored using a drone motor and propeller to move the board. The approach is loud and inefficient, but it proved the concept.

## How it works

The propulsion system is a high-speed brushless motor mounted to the back of the board.
- **Power**: 3S LiPo battery.
- **Propulsion**: A multi-blade propeller displaces air backward to move the board forward.
- **Safety Shroud**: A fan shroud repurposed as a propeller guard to protect the propeller and hands.

## Parts List

- **Motor**: Brushless motor and ESC.
- **Board**: Standard skateboard deck.
- **Remote**: 2.4GHz RC car remote.

## Performance

The board moves, but propeller-driven propulsion has significant limitations. It’s louder than belt or hub motor systems and less efficient. The design served as a first exploration into electric vehicle building using available parts, rather than a practical skateboard design.
